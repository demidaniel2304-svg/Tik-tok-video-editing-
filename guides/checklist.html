Vlog filming checklist

Before you
press record

Four shots · five seconds · feet, not phone

Before you go

  • Wipe the lens
  • Grid on to keep shots level
  • Plan the day arrive → eat → explore → treat → bonus

At every stop: 4 shots

1Wide

The whole place, from far back

2Medium

The action: table, stall, bar

3Close

One detail: food, a sign, a bauble

4Me

Your hand in shot, or the kids from behind

Every shot

  • Stop, frame, record no swivelling while recording
  • Hold for 5 count it in your head
  • Move your feet between shots, not the phone
  • Check the middle is it what you want seen?
  • Lock brightness long-press what matters

Moving shots

  • Only with a purpose follow the kids, or one slow tilt
  • Elbows tucked phone at chest height

At night

Stand still more Face the lights Use 1x, not 0.5x

Bonus

  • 5 seconds of good sound music, chatter, cups clinking
Then put the phone away and enjoy it.One minute of filming per stop is plenty.
