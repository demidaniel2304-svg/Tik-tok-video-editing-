Research

The Dickens Inn

  • A pub and restaurant on the edge of St Katharine Docks, near Tower Bridge.
  • The building is a timber warehouse from the late 1700s. Sources disagree on whether it stored spices or tea.
  • It was moved about 70 metres and reopened as a pub in 1976, opened by a descendant of Charles Dickens.
  • Pub downstairs, restaurant floors above, balconies with flowers outside. The menu includes fish and chips and Sunday roasts.

Sources: CAMRA London, Tower Hamlets, Londonist

The Galeón Andalucía

  • A full-size replica of a 17th-century Spanish galleon, the kind of ship that sailed Spain's trade routes. It's a replica of a type of ship, not of one particular named galleon.
  • Moored in St Katharine Docks for winter: open daily from 14 November to 10 January, closed Christmas Day.
  • Hours 10:00–19:00, Christmas Eve 10:00–14:00.
  • Six decks to explore on board, including the gun decks and sailors' quarters. Free to see from the quayside.
  • Adult tickets from about £12, children around £7. Reports differ, so check before you go.

Sources: St Katharine Docks, Time Out, ianVisits
